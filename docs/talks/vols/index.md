## Computational tools for universal algebra research

Notes for a talk at the 2nd CSP World Congress in Völs am Schlern, Italy on 25 September 2020.

+ **Thank the organizers**

+ **[The Universal Algebra Calculator](http://uacalc.org)**

  - It's available from [uacalc.org](http://uacalc.org/)
  - *If you use* [Linux][]... [some notes](https://williamdemeo.gitlab.io/pl/uacalc/) to get you setup are at [williamdemeo.gitlab.io/pl/uacalc](https://williamdemeo.gitlab.io/pl/uacalc/).  
    *If you don't use* [Linux][]... what's wrong with you?

  - A [repository of algebras](https://github.com/UACalc) readable by the UACalc is available at [github.com/UACalc](https://github.com/UACalc).  
    (Some of the .ua files in that repository were used to solve real life math problems.)

  - What does a .ua file look like? ...and why should you care?
