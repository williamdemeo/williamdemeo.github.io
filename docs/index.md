# William DeMeo

Mathematician and formal verification engineer. I work on machine-checked
mathematics — proving theorems in dependent type theory, verifying production
systems, and building tooling that lets language models work inside a proof
assistant.

## What I'm working on

**Formal verification at IO.** Machine-checked specification of the Cardano
blockchain ledger in Agda, with the Formal Methods team.

**AI tooling for proof assistants.** An MCP server exposing Agda's interaction
protocol, Claude Skills encoding Agda workflow knowledge, and the agent loops
built on top of them.

**[agda-algebras](https://github.com/ualib/agda-algebras).** A library of
universal algebra in Agda, containing a machine-checked proof of Birkhoff's
HSP theorem in Martin-Löf type theory.

## Background

PhD in mathematics from the University of Hawaii, in universal algebra and
lattice theory, under Ralph Freese. Before moving into industry I held research
and teaching appointments at Charles University in Prague, the University of
Colorado Boulder, the University of Hawaii, Iowa State University, and the
University of South Carolina.
